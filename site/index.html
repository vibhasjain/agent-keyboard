Run your own.

A small server, a ~15 KB widget, your repo — the README walks you through it.

$ git clone https://github.com/vibhasjain/agent-keyboard.git
# a free Supabase project does auth — one user: you
$ fly launch --config server/fly.toml
$ fly secrets set CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN=… GH_TOKEN=… \
    SUPABASE_URL=… SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=… SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY=… \
    ALLOWED_EMAIL=you@example.com \
    SITES='[{"id":"mysite","repo":"https://github.com/you/mysite.git","branch":"main","domain":"mysite.com"}]'
# one tag, before </body>, on any page you want to edit:
<script src="https://YOUR-APP.fly.dev/widget.js" data-site="mysite" defer></script>

What's in the bar

Small on the page. Serious underneath.

Fire-and-forget

Close the tab — the job keeps running on your server. Reopen and the bar re-attaches to the stream.

Any input

Type it, hold to dictate it, or attach a photo of what you mean.

One tag, ~15 KB

Zero dependencies, Shadow-DOM isolated — it can't clash with your CSS and your CSS can't break it.

Memory

One session per site — it remembers your last ask and self-compacts when long, shown inline as · session compacted ·

An allowlist, not a free-for-all

The server only touches repos you list in its config. It can't wander.

Your keys, your box

Your Claude token, your Supabase, your VM. Nothing phones home to anyone.

Questions

The honest version.

Who can use the bar?

Whoever you allow-list. ALLOWED_EMAIL takes one address or a comma-separated few — just you, or a client you built the site for, a partner, an editor. You create each user by hand in your Supabase; there are no public signups and no multi-tenancy.

An agent pushing straight to main — is that fine?

It pushes to a repo you own, from an allowlist you wrote, with every commit attributed to "Agent Keyboard." Made a change you don't like? Just ask Agent Keyboard to roll it back — no big deal.

What does it cost to run?

Mostly things you already pay for: the Claude Code subscription does the editing, Supabase's free tier does auth, and a small Fly VM (often free, a few dollars a month at most) does the hosting. The OpenAI key is optional and only for voice.

What if I close the tab mid-change?

The job keeps running on the server. Come back and the bar re-attaches.
